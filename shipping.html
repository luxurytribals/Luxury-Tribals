Shipping Information

We want to get your Luxury Tribals products to you as quickly and safely as possible. Here's everything you need to know about our shipping process.

🚚 Free Shipping Available!

Orders above ₹999 qualify for FREE shipping across India

1. Shipping Partners

We ship all orders through Shiprocket, India's leading shipping aggregator. Your order may be delivered by one of their partner couriers including:

  • Delhivery
  • Blue Dart
  • DTDC
  • Ecom Express
  • Xpressbees

The courier is automatically selected based on your location for fastest delivery.

2. Delivery Time

  • Metro Cities: 3-5 business days
  • Other Cities: 5-7 business days
  • Remote Areas: 7-10 business days

Note: These are estimated timeframes. Actual delivery may vary due to local conditions, holidays, or unforeseen circumstances.

3. Shipping Charges

  • Orders above ₹999: FREE shipping
  • Orders below ₹999: ₹99 shipping fee

Shipping charges are calculated automatically at checkout.

4. Order Processing

  • Orders are processed within 24-48 hours of payment confirmation
  • Orders placed on weekends/holidays are processed on the next business day
  • You will receive an order confirmation email immediately after placing your order
  • A shipping confirmation email with tracking details will be sent once your order is dispatched

5. Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive:

  • AWB Number: Your unique tracking number
  • Tracking Link: Click to track your order in real-time
  • SMS Updates: Status updates via text message
  • Email Updates: Shipment progress via email

You can track your order anytime by entering your AWB number on the courier's website.

6. Delivery Address

Important: Please ensure your shipping address is complete and accurate:

  • Full name of recipient
  • Complete address with house/flat number
  • Landmark (if applicable)
  • City, State, and PIN code
  • Active phone number

We cannot be held responsible for delays or non-delivery due to incorrect addresses.

7. Delivery Attempts

  • The courier will make up to 3 delivery attempts
  • Please ensure someone is available to receive the package
  • A valid photo ID is required for delivery verification
  • If all attempts fail, the order will be returned to us

8. Non-Delivery / RTO (Return to Origin)

If your order is returned to us due to:

  • Incorrect address
  • Customer unavailability
  • Refusal to accept delivery

We will contact you to arrange re-shipment. Additional shipping charges may apply for re-dispatch.

9. International Shipping

Currently, we only ship within India. International shipping will be available soon!

10. Damaged or Lost Packages

  • All packages are insured during transit
  • If your package arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours with photos
  • For lost packages, please wait 10 business days before reporting
  • We will investigate and arrange a replacement or refund
